Zomba, Malawi

Nestled at the base of the dramatic 1,800-meter Zomba Plateau, Zomba features a lush, high-altitude landscape of dense pine forests, rolling hills, and cascading waterfalls that offer a cool refuge from the surrounding African plains. As Malawi's former colonial capital and the home of the University of Malawi, the city's culture is a vibrant blend of deep historical heritage, academic energy, and warm Malawian hospitality, which is vividly on display within its bustling central markets and colonial-era neighborhoods. Visitors are drawn to top attractions like the scenic hiking trails and viewpoint vistas of the plateau, the historic Old Parliament building, and the peaceful National Herbarium and Botanic Gardens.
