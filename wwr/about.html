ZOMBA RAFTING EXPERIENCE

Welcome to Zomba Rafting Experience

Founded with a deep passion for outdoor sports and the untamed beauty of Malawi’s old capital city, Zomba Rafting Experience is the premier destination for professional white-water rafting and river safety lessons. Nestled at the base of the majestic Zomba Plateau, we transform the rushing, pristine waters flowing from Mulunguzi Dam into an interactive classroom for adventurers of all skill levels. Our mission is to combine high-adrenaline river sports with world-class instruction, making the thrills of river rafting safe, accessible, and unforgettable for locals and international travelers alike.

Safety and environmental care sit at the core of everything we do. Our team consists of highly trained, certified swift-water rescue instructors who possess an intimate knowledge of Zomba’s river systems. We provide state-of-the-art rafting gear, rigorous safety orientations, and personalized, step-by-step coaching to build your confidence on the water. Whether you are a complete beginner picking up a paddle for the first time or a seasoned kayaker looking to master local rapids, we are here to guide you through every splash.

History

The roots of Zomba Rafting Experience trace back to a shared love for the unique hydrology of Malawi's historical capital city. For generations, the waters cascading down the slopes of the Zomba Plateau, channeled through the Mulunguzi River, were celebrated primarily for their scenic beauty and role in supplying water to the bustling town below. However, in the early 2010s, a small group of local white-water enthusiasts and international rapid-runners recognized that the seasonal, high-altitude torrents offered an untapped, world-class playground for river sports. What began as informal weekend expeditions exploring uncharted, rocky drops quickly evolved into a structured mission to introduce paddle sports to Malawi. In 2021, we officially opened our doors as Zomba Rafting Experience, establishing the country’s first dedicated swift-water instructional program to preserve our rivers through safe, sustainable, and eco-friendly eco-tourism.
