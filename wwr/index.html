Conquer the Rapids of Zomba

Unforgettable White‑Water Thrills

From gentle Class II ripples to heart‑pounding Class IV drops, our certified guides lead you through the most spectacular stretches of the Mulunguzi River. Whether you are a first‑time paddler or a seasoned rafter, we tailor every trip to your skill level.

All our guides are swift‑water rescue certified, and we provide top‑of‑the‑line helmets, life jackets, and rafts. Your safety is our priority – so you can focus on the splash, the scenery, and the pure joy of river rafting.
